How it works

One connected loop, four parties

Every ORCA appliance links the customer, the financing institution, the payment gateway, and the ORCA Cloud — so repayment, device health, and ownership status stay in sync in real time.

Customer
Applies, gets approved, activates the appliance
Financing institution
Underwrites, monitors repayment and risk
Payment gateway
Mobile money, bank transfer, card
ORCA appliance
Reports status, receives control commands
